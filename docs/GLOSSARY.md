# Glossary

- **Embodied AI / 具身智能:** AI whose observations and actions are tied to a body interacting with a physical or simulated environment.
- **Physical AI / 物理人工智能:** A broader industry term for AI that perceives, reasons about, and acts in the physical world.
- **World model / 世界模型:** A model that represents or predicts how relevant parts of an environment evolve, ideally conditioned on actions.
- **VLA (Vision-Language-Action, 视觉—语言—动作模型):** A model that maps visual observations and language instructions to robot actions.
- **Verifier / 验证器:** A system that judges progress, success, constraint satisfaction, or uncertainty.
- **Reward model / 奖励模型:** A learned function that scores how desirable or successful a state or trajectory is.
- **Rollout / 轨迹执行:** One attempt by a policy, including observations, actions, states, timing, and outcome.
- **Embodiment / 本体形态:** The robot's physical form, sensors, joints, actuators, and action space.
- **Cross-embodiment / 跨本体:** Transfer of data, representations, or policies between different robot bodies.
- **Sim-to-real / 仿真到现实迁移:** Training or testing in simulation and transferring the result to physical hardware.
- **Teleoperation / 遥操作:** A human remotely controls the robot, often to collect demonstrations or intervene in failures.
- **Calibration / 校准度:** Whether predicted probabilities match observed frequencies; a 70% success prediction should succeed about 70% of the time.
- **Regression / 回退:** A new model improves one area but becomes worse on a previously working behavior.
- **Data flywheel / 数据飞轮:** Deployment generates data, data improves the model, and the better model enables more deployment and data.
