# V1.23 withdrawn (2026-10-19, before printing)

V1.23 was the unprinted V1.22 ankle parts plus the reworked hot-glue sole molds on the Black plate. The hot-glue route
is dropped: a PLA mold warped under the heat gun, no printed plastic survives a heat gun, and casting a room-temperature
sole is not available to Shawn ("没有浇筑的可能"). The soles go to bike inner tube on thin tape
(`build-pack-20260913/procurement/feet-sole-20261018/README.md`).

Print V1.22 (`../release-V1.22/`, `~/Downloads/zhengxia_MicroDuck_V1.22/`): the same eight ankle parts, Black plate with
the two sleeves only. This directory stays as the record of the mold rework; its Downloads folder is in the Trash and
PARTS-SHEET-V1.23.html is no longer linked from the guide.
