## Findings worth keeping

- **Why V1.8 exits at the back of the head**: the old USB-C plug (about 8×12 mm) could not pass the neck. With UART
  wires, a flat 8-wire group passes the -y slot, which is 4.41–4.55 mm wide at rest. Details: `neck-route/README.md`
  and `integrated/README.md` (route A3).
- **Amp terminal block**: Adafruit links the 4UCON drawing 19963, which gives 8.5 mm tall. That leaves about 2.2 mm
  to the cap, so the earlier 0.71 mm figure was too pessimistic.
  - That the 3006 ships with this block is inference. The 3006 CAD model in Adafruit_CAD_Parts omits the block.
  - The speaker leads must be clamped before the amp is mounted.
- **Pi post**: Shawn broke the left post seen from the front, which is on the duck's -y rail. The likely spot is the
  lower boss E-pi-01 (inference from the stress figures). Option A in `pi-mount/README.md` reinforces both rails and
  needs no new parts.
- **Jaw versus neck**: `jaw-neck-check/README.md` plus `margin-yaw.json`.
  - Straight ahead, the jaw clears up to 40°. A turned head reaches the head-pitch bracket, worst at yaw ±60° and
    ±120° with first contact at 26.25°.
  - A 25° jaw cap clears every yaw, and the trained policy uses at most 18.33°.
  - Separate problem: the simulated ball release tips head_pitch to 71–85° with the jaw open. There the jaw hits
    ID12 and the shells. Rule: head_pitch ≤ 65° while the jaw is open.
  - The simulator has no collision geometry for the neck, motors or shells, so training never saw these contacts.
- **OpenRB firmware compile**: the core's macOS toolchain is x86-only, and Rosetta is not installed. Compile in a
  Linux arm64 container instead. Run `colima start`, then `pack-uart/firmware/compile.sh` with
  `DOCKER_CONFIG=<dir with {}>` and `DOCKER_HOST=unix://$HOME/.colima/default/docker.sock`, then `colima stop`.
  The guide's bridge (Serial2, J12) builds to 12,632 bytes.
- **CAD venv**: `/tmp/microduck-cad-venv` is a symlink to `~/.venvs/microduck-cad` (59 pinned packages,
  `guide/cad-venv-requirements.txt`).
  - Cause: a cleanup at 2026-10-13 00:00 deleted every /tmp file that had not been read recently, including
    pyvenv.cfg and the .py files.
  - The damaged copy is parked at `/tmp/microduck-cad-venv.damaged-20261013` and can be deleted.
- **Records of rejected alternatives**:
  - `pack-uart/` option A: J6 A1/A2 plus a cover window, with an `openrb_uart_bridge_a1a2` firmware that compiles.
  - The full OMX-F 12 V-on-OpenRB chain as a formal plan. Reasons against: the XL4015 (about 51×26×14 mm) does not fit
    the 26×30×8 mm converter bay, it needs an ankle-to-ankle cable under the crotch, it puts 5.4 A peaks on a 3 A pin,
    the Pi gets no 5 V, and 12 V lands on every OpenRB port. It stays a bench-only setup (POWER-12V.html).
