MicroDuck shoot storyboard, 2026-10-24

Top views of the two tables (1.8 m by 1.2 m), the far wall at the top, Shawn's seat at the bottom. Scale 1 m = 160 px. Duck = yellow, path = blue, camera = purple, power bank = grey, hand = pink, orange part = orange.

1 · Walk and arc shoot.sh walk --straight-s 3 --arc-s 3

Duck walks right to left along the middle line (dashed), 3 s straight, 3 s left arc. Path about 0.65 m. The lead comes from the power bank behind it, slack on the table. Shawn's spare hand hovers beside the duck the whole take.

2 · Grip stop shoot.sh grip

Straight walk, up to 8 s. Palm on the head cap stops it; lift 10 to 20 cm, hold 2 s, set down, let go. The arm enters from the near side and stays out of the lower half of the frame: camera a little to the right of the hand so the arm does not cross the lens.

3 · Two quacks shoot.sh quack

Standing still (stand61). Two quacks 3 s apart: jaw opens about 15 deg, a small head tilt, jaw pressed shut after. Beak in the upper third of the frame; 1x or 2x lens.

4 · Crouch to the part shoot.sh crouch

Ground-pick crouch over 2.5 s to the lowest pose (mouth tip 22 mm over the table, 69 mm ahead of the feet), held 4 s, then the rise. Lens as low as the box allows so the beak and the part are both in frame; 2x lens fills the frame better.

5 · Rear pack close-up shoot.sh back

Standing still; the head sweeps left and right over 8 s so the clip is not a photo. Rear converter pack, lead and wires sharp; 2x lens, focus locked on the pack.

Side view · lens height

iPhone 16 Pro, landscape, 4K 30 fps, 1x main lens (2x for the close-ups), no ultra-wide, no cinematic mode. Long-press the duck to lock AE/AF before each take. Record 2 s before "action" and 2 s after the duck has come to rest.
