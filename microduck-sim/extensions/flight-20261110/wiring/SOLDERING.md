# Flight pack: solder joints

Generated by build_wiring.py (2026-11-10). 30 joints (fans ARESWEI HDS001; capacitor legs go straight onto the tabs). Everything else plugs.

- **ESC + tab: right pigtail red, left pigtail red, capacitor + leg** (1 joint(s), 3 conductors). Why not a plug: the ESC has bare battery tabs only; a plug here would be one more XT60 in series carrying both packs (up to 160 A uncapped, 110 A capped), worse than solder
- **ESC - tab: right pigtail black, left pigtail black, capacitor - leg** (1 joint(s), 3 conductors). Why not a plug: same as the + tab
- **XT60 male solder cups on the two pigtails (+ and -)** (4 joint(s), 4 conductors). Why not a plug: XT60 contacts are solder-cup parts; there is no crimp XT60 in the parts list
- **16 AWG fan wires onto the ESC motor pads (4 fans x 3; FL 160 mm, RL 70 mm, FR 40 mm, RR 40 mm)** (12 joint(s), 12 conductors). Why not a plug: the ESC has solder pads only; no connector option on this board
- **3.5 mm female bullets onto the free end of each pad wire** (12 joint(s), 12 conductors). Why not a plug: bullets are solder-cup parts; this joint makes the fan end a plug. A female bullet cannot sit on the pad itself: three 3.5 mm bullets do not fit side by side on a pad set about 4.5 mm apart

Plugged, no solder: XT60 pack-to-pigtail (2), 12 fan bullets, FC-ESC 8-pin, FC-MTF-01 4-pin, FC-A7Z 3-wire lead, balance leads (charger only).
