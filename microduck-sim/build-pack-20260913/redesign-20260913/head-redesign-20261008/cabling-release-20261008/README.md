## Bounded continuation and freeze

Four complete static I2S routes now connect Pi physical pins 12/20/35/40 to the official Eagle amplifier pads. Their lengths are 50.20/57.34/96.90/98.36 mm; all are Ø1.5 mm with R2 mm bends, clear of current solids and one another. They require the fully installed male header plus female housing to extend **at most 11 mm from the PCB component-side face**. The old 15 mm combined housing-and-wire receiver is not an actual connector dimension. Independent Pi components and solder tails remain obstacles. A constant-length service path during cap opening, pin fanouts for the other nets and coexistence with the full FFC remain open.

One finite head-to-neck trunk uses a 10.2×3.2 mm flat receiver for two rows of six Ø1.5 mm conductors. It clears the jaw mechanism, motors and speaker. It needs a local rear cap portal. That closed portal collided during 58 of 161 cap-closing states; opening its lower edge along the demonstrated collision removes another 250.49 mm³ and clears all 161 states. Both candidate shells remain single watertight bodies. This only proves the cap-to-fixed-trunk interface: the lower edge still needs wire protection and restraint, and the internal fanouts remain incomplete. Neither cap variant is released.

The one tested external neck-loop family has constant 180 mm length. Among 229 states, 34 collide and 7 cannot span the anchors; some bends are much tighter than R2 mm. It is **rejected**. Its head trunk plus external segment already requires 271.07 mm before electrical endpoint fanouts, so it cannot establish the existing 250 mm harness. This result does not prove every 250 mm design impossible. The next complete design must use actual available total length, keep at least R2 mm throughout, and clear all 229 states plus assembly, with supported connector loads. These remain engineering tasks, not merely missing measurements.

The purchased **4397** has now been checked against its manufacturer drawing: female DuPont sockets, UL1571 28 AWG wire, **0.8 mm wire OD**, and **150±10 mm flexible span between the plastic shoulders**. Those facts no longer need measurement. The missing installed dimension is the PCB-face-to-female-wire-exit distance after full seating on the owned Pi. The current conditional route requires ≤11 mm.

For SC1892, record the received 16 mm or 11.5 mm revision and the **rigid reinforced portion protruding beyond each closed latch**, plus its total thickness. The manufacturer PCN already resolves nominal width; a generic photo of a blue tab cannot establish where a bend may begin. The Pi-side immediate-bend route remains unaccepted.

The six final head print holds remain. The optical outer eye contour may remain an interface constraint, but neither the whole eye trim nor the face/camera mounts is independently released while the full ribbon layout is unsettled. No canonical or deployed bundle changes accompany this continuation.
