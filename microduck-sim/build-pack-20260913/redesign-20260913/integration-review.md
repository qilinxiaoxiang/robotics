# Independent integration review

Verified 2026-09-13 for P0, P5 and P6. This review compares the final power, compute and mechanical reports with the current runtime and training files. It does not change the canonical contract, authorize purchases or claim physical commissioning.

The Pi-on-robot and Mac-for-vision architecture is the preferred design: its electrical pin assignments, standard USB connections and coverage of all 15 actuators agree. The integrated review found two real reach failures that individual electrical and mechanical reviews had missed: the proposed 100 mm Pi power wires and the original 150 mm IMU cable are shorter than their moving-body routes. The selected power route is now corrected to four 250 mm 22 AWG leads, and the sensor route to a completed 250 mm harness; the final guide and resource list must preserve those corrections. The learned visual-pickup software remains required work; a complete hardware design is not completion of that software.

## Positive checks

- A fresh static check found **12 assigned physical header contacts with no duplicates**. Pi power uses 2/4 and return 6/14; BNO085 uses 1/3/5/9; audio uses 12/35/40/20. Pins 2/4 are the 5 V rail, not programmable signal outputs. Raspberry Pi explicitly allows power through those pins, within 4.75–5.25 V. The audio manufacturer's GPIO18/19/21 assignment agrees with the proposed I2S connections. [Official header power guidance](https://github.com/raspberrypi/hats/blob/master/designguide.md), [official GPIO documentation](https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/computers/raspberry-pi.html#gpio-and-the-40-pin-header), [Adafruit audio wiring](https://learn.adafruit.com/adafruit-max98357-i2s-class-d-mono-amp/raspberry-pi-wiring).
- The eight motor groups in the actual runtime are (1,2), (3,4), (5,10), (6,7), (8,9), (11,12), (13,14), and (15). IDs 1–15 occur exactly once. Thus eight motor-branch fuses are intentional: seven two-motor branches and one jaw branch. The Pi's separate ninth 3 A branch and the audio's 0.5 A branch do not reduce motor coverage. Evidence: [integration-verification.json](integration-verification.json), [runtime/core.py](../phase1/runtime/core.py).
- A new supplier check confirms SC0727 is **Micro-B male to USB-A female, 80 mm**. Its Micro-B plug inserts into the Pi's USB data receptacle. Adafruit 4472 is **USB-A male to USB-C male, 152.4 mm**, with data conductors: its A plug enters SC0727, and its C plug enters OpenRB. No raw USB connector assembly is needed for this data route. Manufacturer information identifies 4472 as a 5 V cable; it is not the 20 V charger cable. [SC0727 specification](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/raspberry-pi/SC0727/15298144), [4472 manufacturer specification](https://www.adafruit.com/product/4472).
- Camera SC1224 and the SC1892 cable remain in the design; both circuit boards supply latch sockets, and the ribbon supplies contact ends. The camera and OpenRB receive power through the Pi branch; the amplifier has its own fused branch. The Mac exchanges data over Wi-Fi and does not add a second duck power source.
- The new regulator insert passes the documented rigid fit check, and the USB cap passes only its declared **25 × 18 × 8 mm received-plug envelope**. The checks explicitly preserve the rejected 9 mm-high plug envelope. The new camera/audio print additions are enumerated, so they can no longer disappear from a generated queue unnoticed. [Mechanical evidence](mechanical-geometry-verification.json), [USB evidence](usb-service-window-verification.json).
- The jaw checkpoint and exported actor freshly match both hashes in the training validation. The original experiment recorded 6,144 training decisions and 12/12 trained successes against 0/12 initial successes. Its inputs use privileged simulation state; no camera training or physical transfer is inferred from these results. [Training validation](../../microduck-simulator/rl/results/validation.json), [training summary](../../microduck-simulator/rl/results/training-summary.json), [fresh hash check](integration-verification.json).

## Seven integration items to close or carry explicitly

1. **Replace the two undersized head-to-torso harnesses.** The fresh 1,500-pose reach check measures regulator-to-Pi straight-line distances of 151.94–156.91 mm and an anchored route up to 162.33 mm. The IMU-to-Pi straight-line maximum is 152.31 mm. Therefore 100 mm Pi power leads and the 150 mm stock IMU lead fail before service slack. The power reviewer has now selected four 250 mm 22 AWG leads and recomputed the endpoint screen. Independent arithmetic gives 4.818062825 V at 1.5 A using the documented resistance allowances, matching the revised calculation; the final conservative check includes regulator/IMU box corners and the whole header housing. Its maximum Pi route is 188.99 mm and IMU route 174.99 mm. After reserving 50 mm for bends and terminations, 250 mm leads retain 11.01 mm and 25.01 mm respectively. The IMU route is also corrected to 250 mm. This closes the known design-length error; actual retention, current sharing, endpoint voltage and routing still require assembly checks. Preserve a flexible neck loop and verify it over the actual full movement envelope. [New reach calculation](harness-reach-review.json).

2. **Make the GPIO assembly a single, physically coherent harness.** A full 2×20 female housing covers the entire Pi header: it must contain all 12 allocated contacts. Do not also instruct the builder to insert separate BNO/audio female plugs into the occupied header. Use contacts documented to fit that housing; if the old 4397 contacts do not match, retain its JST-SH end and about 100 mm of wire, and splice four compatible pre-crimped signal tails to a measured 250 mm final length. The manufacturer color assignment is red → Pi1, blue → Pi3, yellow → Pi5, black → Pi9. This requires four insulated wire splices, not soldering the 1 mm JST-SH connector. Include four audio signal leads, four Pi power contacts, and four IMU contacts in the resource count. The completed housing and lead exit must fit the reviewed 7 × 54 × 15 mm receiving envelope; the 18 mm-high alternatives hit the motor/support and are rejected. This is a CAD receiving limit, not a verified supplier housing size. [Housing check](gpio-housing-review.json). [4397 manufacturer wiring](https://www.adafruit.com/product/4397).

3. **Publish one current capacity statement and retain conservative commissioning limits.** The new source calculation supports a 9 A total design budget, reserving 2 A for non-motor loads; the runtime currently rejects calibration values above 5 A total servo current and trips a fused group above 2.25 A. These are different quantities. Keeping the existing 5 A runtime threshold is a valid conservative starting point; do not silently increase it because the converter has a higher rating. Any later increase requires measured total supply, per-group endpoint and task results, and an updated calibration hash. The mechanical report's older 20 V/3 A and 60 W examples have now been corrected; both subsystem reports agree on fixed 20 V at at least 3.25 A. Preserve that requirement in the final instructions. [Runtime limit check](integration-verification.json), [source budget](power-review.md).

4. **Make the actual print and fabrication handoff match the selected electrical design.** The revised USB cap replaces one existing instance; the regulator insert, camera foot, camera cradle and audio carrier add four, yielding 89 instances if the original 85 remain. Update the generated queue, source hashes, current preview and fastener quantities together. Retain the narrowly sized audio ties, speaker wires, amplifier gain resistor, four digital-audio/ground wires, insulation, converter thermal provision, receiver enclosure and tether strain relief as installed requirements even when club-supplied. The actual SC0727 moulding must be checked against the documented 8 mm-high allowance before closing the head; its published cable length is not a moulding-height specification.

5. **Keep the exact visual-learning work in the delivery contract.** The current Pi runtime accepts only `seq` and `action`, and its pickup action is a fixed calibrated sequence. It does not yet consume images, target poses or the learned jaw actor. The following concrete software chain is still needed: Pi camera capture with calibration identity, frame sequence and capture time; a bounded newest-frame transport to the Mac; Mac target localization and camera-to-mouth calibration; bounded forward/yaw/head requests with explicit frame age and confidence; a Pi supervisor that rejects stale, missing, duplicate or uncalibrated observations while retaining its single 50 Hz motor-bus owner; a real-observation adapter and learned-jaw controller; synchronized trial recording and independent grasp/lift/hold/release assessment. A color/shape target locator on a calibrated support plane is a low-cost automatic baseline. It is not permission to replace automatic alignment with manual placement. Existing camera, motor, IMU and audio scheduling must be measured together on the Pi after installation.

6. **Continue from the existing checkpoint without inventing missing observations.** Preserve `microduck-simulator/rl/models/jaw-ppo.zip` (SHA-256 `ffcdd40c32635db0c9fbdfa32dbca3eb5ee3869b5ded7993115d8d0c1268656d`) and `microduck-simulator/app/public/policies/jaw-ppo.json` (`fda7a44c0787232377da9082cd72ccd66bd7d93184f20069e0b381fc7365e759`). The trained policy uses target position, two opposing contact-force values, external support, lift, root state, jaw state and phase information. A camera can supply some geometric observations but not both measured contact forces. Either add and validate the corresponding real sensing, or continue training with the observations the selected hardware can actually produce, including occlusion and uncertainty. Preserve the original evaluation as a regression baseline. Missing force inputs must not be filled with arbitrary zeros and labeled a validated transfer. Implementation and offline tests can proceed before the hardware arrives; camera calibration, real voltage/timing and physical pickup results require the assembled parts.

7. **Complete the integrated-order and acceptance readback.** Regenerate the full remaining purchase list after the selected revisions, including complete camera/audio modules and their connections, while preserving Pi/SD/header/OTG borrow-first and fabrication club-first rows. Reconcile superseded cart rows and compare whole remaining orders with shipping/tariff treatment. A subsystem subtotal is not the whole order. Mark the new hardware as designed, with physical trials still zero; do not put `single_source_power` into a field interpreted as physically implemented merely because its architecture is complete. Run the sourcing and design checks on the resulting files, and retain a separate full-project acceptance condition requiring real camera-guided learned pickup.

The bounded result is positive for pin and connector compatibility, motor coverage, source-budget architecture and preservation of the actual trained artifacts. The original 100/150 mm harness lengths are rejected and replaced by the reviewed 250 mm routes; the new Pi voltage arithmetic passes its documented design screen. Any claim that the current operator command program already implements the visual-learning task is rejected. The named corrections above make those failures actionable without redefining the user's goal.
