# Closed-head USB: local fit experiment

**Decision to resolve.** Keep Pi as the USB host and OpenRB inside the original curved head. The joint board placement clears its checked rigid neighbors; the USB host adapter has not been confirmed from stock. Test real connector assemblies with two small shell coupons before changing mounts or printing another head. No purchase or adapter selection is authorized by this recipe.

**Fixed datums and available space.** Use the transforms in [coupled-rigid-disposition.json](coupled-rigid-disposition.json). OpenRB stays at its original head datum. Pi is centered at **[59.2, 0, 128.7] mm**, rotated **100° about world Y** from front-up05; its actual header and all 68 modeled board parts move together.

- **Pi Micro-B:** assumed mould-front center **[63.873, −8.911, 143.107] mm**, outward axis **[0.17365, 0, 0.98481]**. The existing **13 × 6 mm** receiving cross-section reaches the original roof after **9.268 mm** along that axis. The current **25 mm** photo-based straight-body model fails. Its mating shoulder and body dimensions are receiving assumptions, not a manufacturer drawing.
- **OpenRB USB-C:** assumed mould shoulder **[−9.087, 0.015, 140.033] mm**, outward axis **[−0.99863, 0, −0.05234]**. The **12 × 7 mm** receiving cross-section reaches the original rear wall after **1.523 mm**. The owned 4472's **22 mm mould plus 5 mm relief** fails here. The 7 mm thickness and 1.2 mm socket-to-shoulder allowance remain explicit assumptions.
- **Conditional USB-A join:** the historical SC0727 + 4472 concept uses a **47.9 × 20 × 12 mm** hypothetical coupled receiving body. SC0727 availability is unconfirmed, so this is **not mandatory owned hardware** and has no accepted position. These local axial limits are section-specific; they are not universally empty rectangular spaces.

**Bench procedure — power disconnected.**

1. Use the actual owned/borrowed connector assembly. The [received-order record](../../../../../procurement/order-101587695-received.json) confirms one 4472; the sourcing policy lists SC0727 as borrow-first, with no confirmed stock. For an alternative, first establish a dimensioned seated shoulder or inspect an already available sample. Prepare two registered coupons from the original closed shell: rear crop **X[−35,−6], Y[−15,15], Z[128,151] mm** and Pi roof crop **X[55,76], Y[−22,4], Z[145,159] mm**. Preserve the real curved inner face. These are fixture crop instructions, not released print files.
2. Locate the board and coupon on an open bench jig using the source PCB mounting holes and frozen transforms. OpenRB's native hole pattern is **20.5 × 61.5 mm**; Pi's is **23 × 58 mm**. Put jig supports outside the plug corridor. Seat the plug fully first, then bring the coupon to its registered position. Record contact location, visible gap, board deflection and whether the connector can be withdrawn after removing the coupon. Do not force the shell onto the plug.
3. Test the complete mated elbow/cable, including its second socket and relief. If retaining SC0727 + 4472, also place the actual A join inside the coupled CAD inventory. A verified Micro-B-host → C-device replacement could eliminate that join, but host role, both mated bodies and cable storage must be established. More cable length alone does not solve either hard-body collision.
4. **Local pass:** fully seated connectors fit beneath both registered curved coupons without shell or board displacement, and a complete internal connection has an accounted-for coupling/exit. Capture its seated geometry for the existing CAD check, then verify mounts, wiring and closure together. A coupon pass alone does not release a populated head. A contact result rejects that specific connector/datum combination and identifies the exact required inward offset or shorter seated projection.

**Why 4432 is not a guaranteed fix.** Its [published overall size](https://www.adafruit.com/product/4432), **23.71 × 19 × 6 mm**, does not define the seated projection, elbow orientation or combined 4472 body. It retains the A join and may still require a checked inward controller adjustment. No specific alternative is approved by the [component review](../../head-layout/usb-component-review/review.json).

**Keep / withdraw.** Retain restoration of the original closed crown and rear curve as the exterior design baseline. Keep the source native registration. Withdraw manufacturing/whole-head acceptance for obsolete Pi/audio mounting features, the assumed straight-plug layout and any unresolved coupling. The [native exterior explanation](../../head-layout/audio-relocation/native/closed-exterior-explanation.json) identifies the old subtraction result as the internal speaker blind pilot; its outer-wall lower bound is **1.02242 mm**. The original exterior restoration is supported independently. It does not adopt the obsolete mounting locations or release the populated head.
