Keep excess on its associated moving link. Use the previously reviewed case tie surface and a loose service loop; do not tie different moving links rigidly together. The CAD curves describe one neutral receiving arrangement, not a prescribed rigid spring shape or flexible-life proof.

## Already assembled legs

Do not try to force plugs through a closed thigh or the ankle/lower-link surround. Reopen the specific original retention needed to return that motor to the preconnection station, keeping unrelated foot/ankle assemblies together where the station permits. A tilted in-place recovery without reopening has not been demonstrated; removing an unrelated black reinforcing plate is not a proven substitute.
