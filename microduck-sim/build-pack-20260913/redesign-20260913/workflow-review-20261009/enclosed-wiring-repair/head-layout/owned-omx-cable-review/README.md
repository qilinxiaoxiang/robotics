# Owned OMX cable-length review — 9 October 2026

The universal 180 mm assumption is not supported by ROBOTIS's own assembly instructions. The current Leader guide uses 100 mm leads for OpenRB → ID1 (step 53) and ID4 → ID5 (step 57), with four 180 mm links. The Follower guide uses 100 mm for OpenRB → ID11 and ID14 → ID15, with three 180 mm links; converter wiring is separate. The older XL4015 and newer converter versions agree on those lengths. [Official Leader data](https://docs.robotis.com/interactive/omx/models/leader.json), [Follower data](https://docs.robotis.com/interactive/omx/models/follower.json), [XL4015 version](https://docs.robotis.com/interactive/omx/models/follower-xl4015.json)

That is **four 100 mm cables used per complete OMX-AI kit**. Three kits correspond to twelve short cables in the official assembly bill; the two kits allocated to MicroDuck correspond to eight. Four belong to the third kit reserved intact for leader/follower experiments and must not be allocated to MicroDuck. These quantities are derived from official assembly use, not an independently verified packing list or a count of currently loose leads. The same two-kit calculation gives fourteen 180 mm links, excluding converter harnesses.

Order 2034348 confirms three kits. Its September shipping status is historical; the current task says the user owns them. Neither source inventories each remaining cable after assembly. The actual available short-lead count therefore remains unspecified rather than being silently set to eight.

Use 100 mm as the first candidate for genuinely short links, retaining enough movement allowance to avoid pulling on the keyed plugs. Stop manufacturing large 180 mm storage folds merely because the old model fixed every edge to 180 mm. This does not prove a specific MicroDuck link accepts 100 mm: connector datums, installed plug length, and the intended motion still determine the fit. No third-kit parts, shopping holds, CAD, or guide data were changed.
