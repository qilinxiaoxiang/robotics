# Limited forward-move result

Moving the existing OpenRB and complete USB-C plug forward by 6, 8 or 10 mm does not enclose the plug. All three retain about 231 mm³ overlap with the original curved cap. This is an actual closed-cap test with the 27 mm official axial length and 12 mm width; the inherited 7 mm thickness remains a declared receiving assumption.

The cap narrows inward at the plug height. The whole-shell minimum X is therefore not a usable plug-clearance bound. Replacing this test with the cap outer bounding box would falsely accept the connector.

The same moves also affect the old cap fasteners and illustrative camera ribbon; they would need coordinated mounting/routing updates, not a global contact waiver. Small intersections with the jaw support are from individually convex-enclosed OpenRB components and still need exact local attribution before claiming real hardware contact.

No part was moved in the canonical assembly. The three cases are retained negative evidence. Next layout work must change a genuine connector-space constraint, not enlarge the rear opening or silently switch the motor-control topology.
