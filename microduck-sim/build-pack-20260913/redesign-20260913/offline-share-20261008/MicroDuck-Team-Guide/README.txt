MicroDuck Team Guide — 8 October 2026

1. Extract the whole ZIP into one folder.
2. Open index.html in current Chrome or Edge. No installation, account, server or configuration is required.
3. The homepage opens the complete assembly and wiring guide. Use the top links for the replacement list and interactive head review.
4. The replacement list Download link opens REPRINT-PARTS.zip containing seven individual prototype STL files.

The pages, pictures, 3D models and JavaScript are included and work locally. External reference links need internet. Keep all files together. Open the extracted HTML, not an HTML preview inside the ZIP.

To check that the guide is ready: the homepage renders the robot and its assembly controls; the replacement list shows 13 numbered parts; the head review renders its 3D model.

The bundle is a dated snapshot. Six camera/audio-dependent parts remain held. Digital prototype checks do not establish physical fit or strength. Follow the statuses on the pages.
