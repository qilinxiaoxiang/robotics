# MicroDuck V1.5 — complete incremental print set

Use the V1.5 package for this printing trip. It replaces the earlier V1.4 and head-only packages. The ZIP contains eight machine files: four colors, with one Bambu X1C alternative and one Elegoo Centauri Carbon alternative for each color. Print each color once, on either matching machine.

All files use PLA and a 0.4 mm nozzle. Bambu files use the Cool Plate profile; Elegoo files use the textured PEI profile. Load the physical filament matching the filename color, and select the corresponding spool or AMS slot at the printer. Each filename starts with `zhengxia_` and includes the native sliced time.

## Twelve pieces

- Yellow: lower head shell, one piece.
- White: eye-camera face panel, one piece.
- Orange: integrated head cap, speaker cradle, speaker retaining ring and IMU holder, one of each.
- Black: eye trim, camera retainer, restored jaw support, mouth sliding ring, neck bracket with integral 1 mm screw seats and IMU rear cover, one of each.

The inventory is incremental against the original 89 printed pieces. It includes eight replacements and four additions. It excludes unchanged body and leg pieces, the retired three Pi spacers, the four separate neck washers, the withdrawn fuse tray and artificial leg cable frames. The converter support and insert already in the original stock are reused.

## Assemble from the current guide

Use the [current assembly guide](http://today.tailfc7c94.ts.net:8766/LEFT-LEG.html). Keep the cap open while mounting and preconnecting the speaker, amplifier, OpenRB and Pi. The Pi uses the cap's integral rails and its existing component-side 40-pin header. Attach the camera to the eye panel. Connect inaccessible plugs before closing covers, arrange ordinary flexible wires through the available passages, and leave slack near plugs and moving joints.

Remove accessible printing supports before installing hardware, including any shallow strand at a screw-hole mouth. The new cap mounting features use eight M2×6 and four M2.5×8 self-tapping screws. Keep the existing fasteners for other unchanged interfaces. No M2×5 is introduced by this package.

This complete print inventory is for the agreed print-and-fit build. The owned straight USB-C housing still conflicts with closed-head placement; a suitable internal Pi-to-OpenRB lead and populated closure require physical fitting. Do not force the shell against a plug. File completeness does not establish powered operation or physical fit.
