# MicroDuck build pack

Revision: 2026-09-13. Scope: one supervised prototype with **15 XL330 servos, including the jaw**.

## Assembly

Open the [illustrated assembly manual](assembly/index.html), or use the editable [Markdown manual](assembly/ASSEMBLY.md). The manual links the current print list, part files, wiring figures and first-power procedure. Follow the current manifest rather than mixing parts from older revisions.

The jaw motor remains part of the design. Fourteen separately purchased servos plus the servo included in the OpenRB Starter Kit provide fifteen total. The updated jaw support corrects the offset between the printed support and the jaw axis. Geometric clearance checks do not constitute a physical assembly or motion acceptance test.

## Power and first operation

Read [power purchase readiness](power/PURCHASE-READINESS.md) and the [first-power procedure](power/FIRST-POWER-TEST.md) before energizing the assembled robot. The documents separate calculations, offline fault tests and measurements.

Hardware power measurements performed: **zero**. The fitted logic regulator requires loaded-voltage screening; thermal behavior, regenerative voltage peaks and actual stop behavior remain measurement gates. The cell-voltage alarm is audible and does not automatically disconnect the battery. The current pack supports a supervised prototype build, not a claim of a proven plug-together walking robot.
